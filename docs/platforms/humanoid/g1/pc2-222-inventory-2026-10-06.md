## D435i acquisition

The camera identifies as Intel RealSense D435I, USB ID `8086:0b3a`, firmware `5.15.1.55`. Both sysfs and librealsense report a USB 2 link (`480` Mb/s in sysfs; USB descriptor `2.1`). No existing camera users were visible to the `unitree` account before acquisition; this was not a privileged inspection of every process.

Using the existing system Python 3.10 binding, the probe opened **640×480 RGB8 and 640×480 Z16 depth at 15 fps**, discarded five warm-up framesets, and received 20 distinct frames from each stream. Measured paired-frame delivery was **14.99 fps** over 1.267 seconds. Both streams reported the `global_time` timestamp domain; synchronization with robot telemetry was not tested.

Depth scale was approximately **0.001 m per raw unit**. The last raw depth frame had **94.52% nonzero pixels**. Software alignment to the color stream succeeded and produced 640×480 depth with **99.62% nonzero pixels**. This establishes acquisition and functioning alignment, not geometric accuracy, object recognition, or usable grasp poses. Image content was not visually inspected. No images were retained.

Sensor-reported RGB/depth intrinsics and depth-to-color extrinsics were captured in the [camera result](/docs/artifacts/g1_pc2_222_20261006/camera.json). Those are internal camera calibration values. The transform from camera to G1 pelvis/planning frame still needs validation. USB 3 operation and higher-bandwidth profiles were not tested.
